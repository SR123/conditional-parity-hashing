An almost-entropic Ingleton score below −0.09738

Conditional hashing of a binary parity structure

Søren Riis · Creator and coordinator

Read / download PDFDOI and archived packageGitHub repositoryLean proof source

Abstract

We prove an improved lower bound of 0.097387436922 on the largest normalised Ingleton violation among four-variable almost-entropic vectors. Previously reported constructions reach approximately 0.0925, used as a rounded benchmark; the best published universal upper bound is 19/124 ≈ 0.15322581.

The construction combines local parity labels, one common binary linear hash, simultaneous conditional privacy amplification and joint conditioning. Applied to a 24-atom source of Matúš and Csirmaz, it subtracts a multiple of the uniform-matroid rank function U(3,4) from all fifteen entropy coordinates. The Ingleton expression is preserved while the joint entropy decreases. The paper gives a general six-test criterion, a complete proof and the finite-alphabet existence conclusion.

Verification and credit

The main theorem has been formalised and kernel-checked in Lean, including the closed rate interval, the score enclosure and finite-alphabet distributions with signed score below −0.09738. The package includes 28 Lean modules, an axiom audit of 281 declarations, pinned dependencies and exact numerical checkers. The finite sampler and fixed-path optimality argument are written proofs rather than separate Lean declarations.
